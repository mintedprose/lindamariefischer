International Women's Day is here - celebrate the joys and challenges of being a woman with my curated playlist on Pandora.

If you're a Pandora listener, go straight in. If not, it's free and easy to sign up.

Here's the backstory:

  • In The Roar of the Butterfly beauty beats its wings to be free.

  • Trails in My Life leaps to twists and turns that come as we run, sometimes in the sun, sometimes in the night.

  • The frustrations in life can lead to gambling with it for the right price—freedom. Sometimes it seems like life on a Map of Ice.

  • Carrying the Nest continues the notion that sometimes we have to strike out on our own. It’s not easy.

  • Tethers Unbound bets on the curative power of time.

  • Thinness of the Air shows that love can sweep you off your feet and into the stars. Times goes on, we age, but our spirit is stronger than ever.

  • In A Material Ghost, we face a world wanting to forget us. But we will not have it. We fought to get here. We belong here.

  • And finally, an anthem for all women, A Female Life. It is a journey meant to be savored.


Thanks for listening! Pass along the email to people you think might like it and thanks again.
