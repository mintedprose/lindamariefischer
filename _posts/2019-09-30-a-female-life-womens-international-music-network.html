A Female Life, a single from Linda Marie Fischer’s Passages album, was covered by Women’s International Music Network.

“I’m very excited that the Women’s International Music Network ran a new story and featured the song on their site,” said Linda Marie Fischer. “Their publication reaches broadly across the globe.”

A Female Life can be heard on Bandcamp, Spotify, and Apple Music, among others.
