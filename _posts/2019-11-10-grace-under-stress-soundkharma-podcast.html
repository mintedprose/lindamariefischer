Sound Kharma Music Podcast included “Grace Under Stress” in its “best new music releases” (episode 51)

Grace Under Stress, the new single from Linda Marie Fischer, was among the featured tracks in episode 51 of Sound Kharma’s podcast. The song, a lyrical ballad, is a patriotic reminiscence of September 11.

“The song spotlight within Sound Kharma is focused on showcasing new artists and music, so I was very excited to be part of this great program,” said Linda Marie Fischer. “I hope it leads people to listen to more of my songs, and those of the other artists featured in the episode as well.”

Listen here to part of “Grace Under Stress”, which starts close to 24:05. It’s towards the end of the podcast where the moderator does a speed round of notable listens.
