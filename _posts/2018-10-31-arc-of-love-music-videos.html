Videos provide yet another medium for the expression of the songs in Arc of Love. I think you'll find the lyric videos for Blasted, Bombed, Being in Love and Racing to the Moon especially entertaining for the way in which they visually complement the different themes that you hear in the music.

Don't forget to check out the other lyric videos for Love in the Ages, Locking Us Forever, Thinness of the Air, and more. They're very nice as well.
