Arc of Love was reviewed in Take Effect Reviews! The album was called “a very elegant listen.”

Referring to singer Linda Marie Fischer, the reviewer said, “Often with a strong beat and fluttering keys, her soft and tuneful vocals guide us through a very mature and heartwarming record that moves and glides with grand sophistication.”
