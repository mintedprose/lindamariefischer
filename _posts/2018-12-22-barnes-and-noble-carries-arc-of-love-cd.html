Barnes & Noble is carrying Arc of Love in its stores and online. The physical CD is available at the Union Square location at 33 E. 17th Street and in Tribeca in lower Manhattan at 97 Warren Street in NYC.

We want to thank Barnes & Noble for supporting the independent artist and giving their shoppers a unique access to new and interesting music.
