All Access Music is a hub for all things music, coast to coast, and places in between, so it’s truly a pleasure to announce that they had an in-depth feature about Arc of Love and the musical inspirations behind the album. In the interview I talk about how my life experiences influenced the lyrics and name a few of my favorites (The song Map of Ice is my top list.).

My talented co-lyricist Phil Fischer and musical director Doug Oberhamer are also recognized for their valuable contributions in the creation of this album. I am working with both on my new releases for 2019. Speaking of those, the article ends with a few hints at what new music we have in store for 2019 (electronic dance, a cross over R&B rap.) It will be exciting to release those songs in the future!
