I was thrilled that LA Music Critic’s Indie Voice Blog called Arc of Love “relaxing and comforting.” That’s right in line with our hopes here and in sync with those who have enjoyed the music.

The blog reviewer also said, “These songs reflect on the universal truth of love and all its phases, and the gentle musical accompaniment helps the words sink in.”

The reviewer pointed out that Arc of Love is great for fans of “Ingrid Michaelson, Sara Bareilles, Vanessa Carlton or Barbra Streisand.”
