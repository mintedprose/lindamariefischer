A Female Life, a single from Linda Marie Fischer’s Passages album, and Grace Under Stress, from her forthcoming Places album, were recently featured on GIGsoup.

“Both songs are happening messages,” Fischer said. “In A Female Life, women are supposed and go out and get it, don’t worry about time that’s past. And in Grace Under Stress, I remember the heroism of those when New York was attacked on 9/11.”

Both songs are available on Pandora, Bandcamp, Spotify, and Apple Music, among others.
