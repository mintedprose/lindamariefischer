My husband Phil and I have abandoned gifts for Valentine’s Day but we do have a rule about giving each other a card. No matter how busy we are, that’s a must on the day. Come in without it and there’s trouble to be had.

For the past few years, he had found the same card, one with a man and woman, or rather a dapper frog and his equally attractive wife. It brought me good laugh to get that a few times (yes, he really did find the SAME card at least three years in a row!)

So you can imagine how pleasantly surprised I was opened the last Valentine’s Day card and it was a little heart in the center of a plain white card. Notwithstanding dapper frogs, sometimes a little heart is all you need!
