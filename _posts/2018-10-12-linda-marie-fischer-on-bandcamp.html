I'm excited to let you know Arc of Love is on Bandcamp, a popular online music store. All of the tracks are individually available there.

What I love about Bandcamp is that it provides such a lovely presentation of the album. Not only are the tracks presented with short descriptions, but it also provides the opportunity to post videos for each song, conveniently located near the track.
