Girls Behind The Rock Show is a cool site all about advancing women in music so I was thrilled to have my musical journey toward my first album, Arc of Love, featured here! I started with a small turntable as a child and despite the various twists and turns of life, I was able to pursue music. My songs capture the complexity of living and loving. The site also has one of my newly released videos, Love in the Ages, for viewing.

Check out the article at Girls Behind The Rock Show and thanks for supporting my message of hope and exploration. It’s a big world out there with lots of opportunities at every age!
