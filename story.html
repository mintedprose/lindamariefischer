Roots Flourish

In my roots are Sicilian musicians. I followed this tradition, singing and acting at a young age. But like many, life took its own turn. As I set off in new directions, I never let go of singing and it never let go of me. Finally the time was right to pursue music. Arc of Love, my first album describes the stages we go through to find love. Sounds easy but it’s anything but, with too many twists and turns along the way.

Passages and Places are my two new albums, both of which reflect long standing concerns and interests of mine. Passages is about challenges we face moving through life, especially as women. And Places touches on certain places we are marvelous and magical.

I owe many who have helped in the production of Arc of Love, Passages, and Places, especially my award-winning musical director and composer Doug Oberhamer. The moods, alternating between hope and hardship, longing and fulfillment, and finally satisfaction and commitment, are set to Doug’s captivating music. The songs are those of the heart; we hope you will find that they speak to you.

Love, Linda
