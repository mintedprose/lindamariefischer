Arc of Love is an album of easy listening/adult contemporary music. It captures loving moments from a life well-lived in all of its complexity. I sing about the thrill at the start of new love, the longing, the struggles, and finally the mutual respect, and commitment. This was my arc of love. But it’s more than just my voice—it tells the story of many who have travelled the long path of love with their own unexpected twists and turns. We fellow travelers know stepping into love is not for the faint-hearted. I hope my songs speak to that great audience.

The songs range from tranquil and smooth to energetic rhythms. The music was influenced by pop, R&B, blues, and jazz. It was created for you to reflect on love gained, love lost, and to dance away the melancholy of time gone by.

I teamed with award-winning composer and musician Doug Oberhamer and co-lyricist Phil Fischer to create Arc of Love and we’re delighted to share it with you.
